Privacy Policy

How we handle information on The AML Desk.

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

The AML Desk is committed to respecting your privacy. This website is designed as a static educational resource. We do not require you to create an account, and we collect minimal information to ensure the site functions correctly.

Data We Do Not Collect

We do not actively collect personal data such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers unless you explicitly provide them by contacting us via email. We do not use third-party analytics trackers or tracking cookies.

Local Storage (Quiz Progress)

Our quizzes and flashcards feature uses your browser's localStorage to save your progress (scores and completed modules) so you can resume where you left off. This data is stored entirely on your device, is never transmitted to any server, and is completely anonymous.

You can clear this data at any time by clearing your browser's site data/cache.

Hosting and Infrastructure

This website is hosted on third-party infrastructure (e.g., GitHub Pages, Netlify, or Vercel). These hosting providers may collect standard server logs, including IP addresses and browser user-agent strings, for security and operational purposes as part of their standard service terms.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time if we introduce new features (such as a newsletter). Any changes will be posted on this page.
