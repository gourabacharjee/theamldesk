About The AML Desk

Why this site exists, who built it, and what we're trying to accomplish.

Our Mission

Why this site exists

When I started studying AML/CFT concepts — preparing for compliance interviews, reading FATF reports, trying to make sense of the alphabet soup of acronyms — I found that good educational resources were either locked behind paywalls, written in dense legalese, or scattered across dozens of regulator websites.

The AML Desk is the resource I wish I'd had when I started. It gathers the essential AML/CFT concepts, global regulations, real-world cases, and career guidance into one place — explained in plain English, free of charge, accessible to anyone with an internet connection.

This is not a compliance program. This is not legal advice. This is an educational resource, built by a learner for learners — because understanding how money laundering works is the first step to stopping it.

"Understand money laundering. Not to do it — to stop it."

Our Approach

Content philosophy

📝

Plain English

Every concept is explained without assuming prior legal or compliance knowledge. Every acronym is defined on first use.

📚

Properly Sourced

Regulations, statistics, and case facts are cited from official regulatory publications and public enforcement actions.

🛡️

Safety-First

Typology descriptions never exceed the generality of public regulator advisories. No operational instructions for evading detection.

🆓

Free & Open

No paywalls, no mandatory sign-ups, no premium tiers. AML education should be accessible to everyone.
